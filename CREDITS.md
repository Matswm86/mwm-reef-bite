Fish scan sources checked on 2026-10-10 and **not used**:
- **Smithsonian 3D Digitization open access** (CC0 per item): the site (3d.si.edu) and its API refused scripted access (HTTP 403 / no response), so I could not confirm any whole-fish specimen or its per-item licence. Museum specimen scans would also be faded, fin-collapsed and the wrong species, so they would need a full repaint and retopology anyway.
- **Kyushu University specimen models on Sketchfab**: mostly CC BY 4.0 (attribution), not CC0. Excluded by the CC0-only rule.
- Individual Sketchfab fish scans found (for example a bluegill): CC BY-NC. Excluded (non-commercial).

## Not used for visible game art

- **Kenney** (CC0, checked 2026-10-10 at https://kenney.nl/support): licence is fine, but the low-poly toy style breaks the owner's "modern realistic, not toy" rule. Allowed only as invisible placeholders.
- **Poly Pizza**: licences differ per model (some CC0, many CC-BY 3.0 from the old Google Poly set). Brief says CC0 only, and the style is low-poly. Not used.
- Nothing from `projects/fishy/` (original XGen Flash art, sound, code). See GDD section 0 and 14.

Godot Engine (MIT) and its third-party notices are shown at runtime on the settings panel ("Lisenser"), built from `Engine.get_license_text()`, `Engine.get_copyright_info()` and `Engine.get_license_info()`, together with the Fredoka OFL text.
