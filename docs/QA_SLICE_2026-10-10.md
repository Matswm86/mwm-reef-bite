# QA: MWM Reef Bite world-1 vertical slice (2026-10-10)

**Verdict: NEEDS-CHANGES.** Nothing blocks a sideload. Lint, load, the logic test, CI, the APK contents and the licence audit all pass, and no screen is blank or cut off. Two things keep this from SHIP for a 4-year-old:
- **Touching the food does not work.** The fish swims to a point 120 px above the finger. A child who puts a finger on the fish it wants to eat clears level 2 in only 8 of 20 sim runs within 4 minutes. With the aim on the finger itself, the same child clears it 20 of 20 times, median 26 s. The softer controls in `b74ba3f` made this worse: it was 17 of 20 before.
- **The player fish gets lost in loud orange kelp.** In the measured frames the side bands are 66% golden-orange pixels, against 0.8% in the approved mock. The scene has 2.8 times the mock's strong edges, and the player's lit flank is under 3:1 against its surroundings in 24 of 30 samples.

The phone performance reading is also still missing.

Audited commit: `ab7c6e5` (HEAD of `main`, which includes the softer controls `b74ba3f` and the README screenshots). The first pass ran on `98fd470`. After the two commits landed during QA, every dynamic check was run again on `ab7c6e5`. Where the two differ, both numbers are given. This report and `tests/qa_probe.*` are the only later commits.

## 4. Touch targets and the lowered home disc (judged)

The cutout shift is `dy = safe_top - 36` (`RbMain.gd:117-125`). The home hit rect becomes x 0-216, y 0 to 216+dy. `in_steer_area` only excludes the fixed 232x232 square (`RbPlay.gd:222-226`), and `RbPlay._input` does not mark steering touches as handled. So any touch at x < 216 and y 232 to 216+dy both steers and arms the home guard. Probe (Xvfb, fake 120 px inset, level 1):
```
home hit rect [P: (0.0, 0.0), S: (216.0, 300.0)]  disc centre (104.0, 188.0)
  control tap on the disc release 1: home guarding=true screen=play
  control tap on the disc release 2 (0.85 s later): screen=map
touch at (150.0, 285.0) in_steer_area=true
  steer touch in the pond down: finger_down=true aim=(150.0, 256.0)
  steer touch in the pond release 1: home guarding=true screen=play
  steer touch in the pond release 2 (0.85 s later): screen=map
```
**Judgement:** this is the same defect as Orb Fence finding 2, and it is not acceptable as is (finding 4). On a 20:9 phone the inset is real (about 100-140 px), and a child steering in the top-left corner twice within 0.3-2.0 s leaves the level. Leaving costs nothing, so this is Medium, not a block.

## 6. Readability and look: the kelp versus the player (judged with numbers)

First, a correction to the brief: **level 2 has no kelp hiding patches** (`RbLevels.gd`, L2 `"kelp": []`). The orange reeds in `03_l2_mid_rings.png` are the **background kelp forest**: GLB MultiMesh at z -2.5 to -30 (`RbWorld.gd:311-344`). The on-plane hiding patches appear only in L4 and L5 (09), and they are louder still. The concern holds for both.

Measurements (`tools/readability.py`, `bands.py` and `camo.py` in the QA scratch folder; my own metrics, not a studio standard). Side bands are x 0-300 and 780-1080 at y 300-1250. "Golden" means hue 20-55 degrees, S > 0.45, V > 0.30.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Side bands, warm-hued (hue 15-60, S > 0.25) | **0.8%** | **66.2%** | 64.4% | 73.7% |
| Side bands, mean chroma C* | 17.1 | 26.7 | 26.0 | 31.1 |
| Pond, strong luminance edges (Sobel > 0.25) | **14.5%** | **40.5%** | 40.2% | 34.7% |
| Pond, golden pixels | 0.0% | 24.4% | 23.3% | 36.5% |
| Open mid-water colour (560, 760) | #127186, lum 0.137 | #68909C, lum 0.252 | | |

`refmatch/look.py` 03 vs the mock gives `FAIL 27.8/100, local contrast x1.61, detail x2.80`. That tool is built for classic originals, so here it only shows the size of the drift.

Player against what is around it (30 samples, a bot playing L2, L4 and L5, a shot every 1.5 s, annulus 0.8-1.6 body lengths around the known player position; clean player colour L\*a\*b\* 66/0/56, lit-flank luminance 0.499):
```
MEDIAN n=30: golden surround 35.8%   lum 2.33:1
lum < 3:1 in 24/30;  golden surround > 25% in 18/30
mock: player vs surround 3.16:1, golden surround 0%
```

**Judgement: the concern holds.**
- **Size:** the player is 92 px long at tier 2 (8.5% of the width; about 40 px in the 540 px README shot, as noted), and it shares its hue family with a third of what surrounds it.
- **Contrast:** it misses DESIGN 3 rule 1 (lit flank >= 3:1 against the water behind the play plane) in 24 of 30 samples.
- **Gold:** the frame breaks DESIGN 3 "the player's gold is the only saturated warm yellow".
- **Clutter:** the frame has 2.8 times the mock's strong edges.
- **The mock does it right:** it uses the same GLBs, but its kelp reads as dark teal-olive silhouettes in fog, and the gold fish is the only warm thing on screen.

Likely causes (for graphic-designer to confirm):
- `rb_kelp_glb.gdshader:36` adds a warm `BACKLIGHT = a.rgb * vec3(1.1, 0.95, 0.65)`.
- Kelp `brightness` is 0.8 (`RbWorld.gd:341`).
- Near kelp sits at 11.5-19 m from the camera, so it gets only about 6-37% depth fog (my calc).
- The play water renders about twice as bright as the DESIGN token #2A6875.

Crop evidence: `r2/player_crops.png` (mock vs L5 kelp vs L5 inside a patch).

The **L4 and L5 hiding patches** add three problems (finding 3):
- **Not the sculpted kelp:** they are the procedural flat-quad frond (`_make_kelp_mesh` with `_kelp_material(true)`, `RbWorld.gd:650,684,697`), untextured orange polygons.
- **Too wide:** the drawn canopy is about 300 px wide (read off 09), against the 160 px safe rect (GDD 5.2), so leaves show where the child is not safe.
- **Leaves in front of the fish:** strands sit at z -0.3 to +0.22 (`RbWorld.gd:691`), in front of fish bodies at +-0.15. The leaves draw over the player, which is the layer rule DESIGN 2 forbids for everything except the plane itself.

Top 5 "prototype" tells, by visual gain per GPU ms:
1. **Orange near-kelp walls.** Pull them toward the mock: teal-olive, more fog, no warm backlight tint. Cost: 0 ms.
2. **Bright play water.** Darken it to #2A6875 so the gold fish reaches 3:1. Cost: 0 ms.
3. **Flat procedural hiding patches.** Use the sculpted kelp GLB, clip the canopy to the safe rect and keep it behind the plane. Cost: about 0.
4. **Thin, scattered surface caustic lines over the top third** (03, 13). They read as a white grid. Soften them or cut their contrast. Cost: 0 ms.
5. **Bobler icons overlap the HUD pill edge** (10, x 900-1040, y 160-216). Tuck them inside the pill. Cost: 0 ms.

DESIGN 11 budget:
- 0 shadowed lights
- 1 particle system (150)
- 9 transparent or scissor shaders
- 0 textures above 2048, but 6 uncompressed 2048 atlases
- SSAO, SSR and fog: no SSAO or SSR; depth and height fog are on, as designed

## 8. Findings

1. **High (child input):** `scripts/RbBalance.gd:42,203-210` (`FINGER_LIFT` 120, `aim_from_finger`). A child who presses on the fish it wants to eat parks the player 120 px above it.
   - Sim: L2 cleared 8 of 20 within 4 min (17 of 20 before `b74ba3f`). The same child with the aim on the finger clears 20 of 20, median 26 s.
   - Fix, game-designer's call. Either snap the aim to an edible fish when the touch lands within about 80 px of it (target assist), or drop the lift in Lett and keep it in Vanlig. Re-run `QA_PHASE=kids` afterwards.
2. **High (readability):** the background kelp forest (`scripts/RbWorld.gd:311-344`, `shaders/rb_kelp_glb.gdshader:29-36`) and the bright play water make the 92 px player hard to find. The numbers are in section 6: 66% warm side bands vs 0.8% in the mock, 2.8 times the strong edges, the player under 3:1 in 24 of 30 samples, refmatch 27.8. Fix for graphic-designer: match the mock. Desaturate and darken the near kelp toward teal-olive, drop the warm backlight tint, raise near fog, and darken the play water to #2A6875. Then re-measure with the same scripts. Target: player >= 3:1 in at least 27 of 30 samples, side bands under 20% warm.
3. **Medium (readability, L4/L5):** `scripts/RbWorld.gd:660-705`. The hiding patches are flat procedural orange fronds, about 300 px wide against a 160 px safe rect, and their strands at z up to +0.22 cover the player. Fix: sculpted kelp mesh, canopy within the safe rect plus a little margin, strands at z <= -0.2, and the player drawn over the leaves.
4. **Medium (child input):** `scripts/RbMain.gd:117-120` together with `scripts/RbPlay.gd:222-243`. With a camera cutout, the lowered home hit rect overlaps the pond (y 232 to 216+dy, x < 216). One steering touch arms the home guard, and a second within 0.3-2.0 s opens the map (probe in section 4). Fix: call `get_viewport().set_input_as_handled()` in `RbPlay._input` on a steering touch-down, or clip `home.size.y` to 232 and widen it sideways.
5. **Medium (VRAM):** the six 2048 atlases in `assets/models/{fish,kelp}/textures/` ship lossless (`"vram_texture": false`) at full size. The DESIGN 4b `size_limit` 1024 for normal and ORM is not applied, because `*.import` is gitignored (`.gitignore:5`). That is about 128 MB of VRAM (my calc), too much for the 32-bit tablet. Fix: set VRAM Compressed and the size limits in those six `.import` files, and commit them (un-ignore that folder) so CI uses them.
6. **Medium (perf):**
   - The win card renders the world twice: `RbWinCard.gd:126` `UPDATE_ALWAYS`, 76 Høy / 73 Lav vs the 60 Lav ceiling. Update the picture at 15 fps or once.
   - Map (163) and settings (194) are 2D-draw heavy. Bake the static disc and trail drawings once into a texture (a SubViewport with `UPDATE_ONCE`, as the HUD already does).
   - Lav play spikes reach 70-76.
7. **Low (licence):** `CREDITS.md:51` promises the Godot licence text at runtime, but no screen shows it (`grep get_license_text scripts/` is empty). Add a small "Lisenser" view on the adult settings panel.
8. **Low (pacing):** with the softer controls, a random-finger child needs 74 / 161 / 156 s (median) for L1 / L2 / L3 Lett, and 2 of 20 runs do not clear L3 within 4 min. GDD 2 aims for 15 s to 2 min. The bot medians doubled (L1 Lett 6.3 to 11.9 s). This is acceptable for an aiming child once finding 1 is fixed; re-check after.
9. **Low (look):** HUD bobler icons overlap the pill edge (10). Caustic lines over the top third read as a white grid (03, 13).
10. **Low (spec):** DESIGN 11 plans MultiMesh or instancing per species for fish. Each fish is its own node and MeshInstance with no pool (`RbPlay.gd:483-489`). It is within budget now; pool them before worlds 2-6.
11. **Low (test tooling):** `tests/capture.gd` passes screen px where it means design px. At 1080x2400 it fails 3 checks (aim lift, wrist strip, home square) that are artefacts, not game bugs. Convert with `play.to_design` or use `test_touch`. Its "home square" check also does not cover the lowered home rect, which would have caught finding 4.
12. **Info (store):** the manifest requires `android.hardware.vulkan.version`. That is fine for a sideload; make it not-required before a Play release. The build is debuggable and debug-signed.
13. **Info (privacy):** the commit author email (a personal address) is public on this public repo, as in every studio repo (Orb Fence too). Consider a GitHub noreply address studio-wide. The public docs also name internal paths (`projects/fishy/`).
14. **Info (test tooling):** runs end with "ObjectDB instances leaked" and "4-6 resources still in use", as in Orb Fence.
